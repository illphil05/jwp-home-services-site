About JWP Home Services

30+ years of experience. Still happy to take the small jobs.

JWP Home Services is built on more than 30 years of hands-on experience fixing, building and caring for homes. Through it all, one thing has remained the same: homeowners need someone they can trust to show up, do quality work and treat their home with respect. That is what JWP Home Services is all about.

As a licensed general contractor, JWP has the experience to handle a wide range of home repairs and improvements, but you do not need a major renovation to give us a call. We are just as happy helping with the everyday projects that come with owning a home. A door that will not close properly. A TV that needs mounting. Sheetrock that needs patching. A shower door giving you trouble. A house that could use a good power washing. Or maybe you simply have a list of things that need to get done. We're here to help.
